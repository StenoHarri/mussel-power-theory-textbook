Also sneaking in some theory

Initials and finals actually don't get dropped in the write-out

Example: "adjourn"

The vowel can be phonetic
A/JUR/-N
or spelling based:
A/JOUR/-N

Initial vowels *can* be dropped, so long as there's no conflict
JOUR/-N -> adjourn
